Deers deers deers

You’ve crashed your car and are now stranded in the middle of nowhere. No signal. No supplies. No help coming. After wandering for hours, you find an abandoned house that might keep you alive.

Hunt, kill, survive. The deer are infinite. No amount of slaughter could keep this population down.

In this BLOCKBUSTER created by Solid Steel Games, you step into the role of a lone deer hunter fighting to stay alive in the wilderness.

Gameplay

Explore open wilderness filled to the brim with unpredictable deer. Go head to head with feral evil deer! Sit down in some shelter and have a nice rest. The possibilities are endless, and it’s all your choice!

Survive 8 nights in the outdoors, and you win.

Compete for the best scores on the leaderboard!

  • Scary survival hunting experience
  • Semi-deer behavior with alert and flee responses
  • Spectacular Sound Design by the Solid Steel Audio Team
